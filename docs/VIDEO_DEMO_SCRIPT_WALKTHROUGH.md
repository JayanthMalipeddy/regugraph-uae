## 3. Anticipated Jury & Stakeholder Q&A Defense Script

### Q1: "How do you guarantee that your AI doesn't hallucinate financial calculations or fee caps?"
**Defense Answer:**
> *"We enforce strict architectural segregation between language synthesis and financial arithmetic. Large language models in ReguGraph-UAE are never permitted to perform math. When a loan contract is audited, Node 1 extracts the numerical parameters, and Node 3 routes them to pure Python IEEE-754 arithmetic functions. The DBR, exit fee caps (1% or AED 10k), and restitution deltas are computed deterministically. The LLM only formats the surrounding legal text around pre-computed, verified numbers."*

### Q2: "Why did you isolate the Diagnostics and Telemetry onto Port 5174 instead of keeping it in the main dashboard?"
**Defense Answer:**
> *"In enterprise banking demonstrations, audience roles dictate interface requirements. Executive stakeholders and retail borrowers need a clean, uncluttered fintech interface focused on facility health and restitution. Risk engineers and central bank auditors, however, need deep observability into vector distances, real-time daemon pings, and benchmark evaluation runs. By isolating diagnostics onto Port 5174 with a reciprocal navigation bridge, we provide both audiences with optimal experiences without compromising system integrity."*

### Q3: "What prevents the platform from incorrectly applying UAE regulations to offshore or foreign loans?"
**Defense Answer:**
> *"Node 5 of our LangGraph workflow is an Autonomous Refusal Gate. It actively inspects the contract's governing law and jurisdiction clauses. If a facility references English Law, the UK Financial Conduct Authority, or foreign dispute forums without CBUAE governance, the agent triggers an immediate refusal state. It halts the audit and generates an explanation notice, completely eliminating regulatory cross-contamination."*

### Q4: "How does your Hybrid RRF Retrieval outperform standard vector search?"
**Defense Answer:**
> *"Dense vector embeddings are excellent for conceptual semantics but frequently struggle with exact statutory numbers—such as distinguishing Article 7 from Article 4.2. BM25 keyword matching, on the other hand, is exact on keywords but misses natural language synonyms. By combining ChromaDB dense cosine vectors with Rank-BM25 sparse tokens using Reciprocal Rank Fusion ($k=60$), we achieve 100.0% recall across both conceptual queries and exact statutory citations."*
