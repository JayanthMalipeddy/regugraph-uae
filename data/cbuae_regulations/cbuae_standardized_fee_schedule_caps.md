# Central Bank of the United Arab Emirates (CBUAE)
## Circular No. 8/2020: Standardized Schedule of Fee Caps on Banking Services to Retail Customers

| Fee Type | Central Bank Statutory Cap (Excl. VAT) | Regulatory Clause Reference | Permitted Deviations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal Loan Processing Fee** | Max 1.0% of loan amount or AED 2,500 (whichever is lower) | Circular 8/2020 Art 4.2 | None permitted. |
| **Auto Loan Processing Fee** | Max 1.0% of loan amount or AED 2,500 (whichever is lower) | Circular 8/2020 Art 4.2 | None permitted. |
| **Mortgage Processing Fee** | Max 1.0% of loan amount or AED 5,000 (whichever is lower) | Circular 31/2013 Art 7.1 | Valuation fees charged at actual third-party surveyor cost only. |
| **Early Settlement Fee (All Loans)** | Max 1.0% of outstanding principal or AED 10,000 (whichever is lower) | Notice 3877/2019 & Circular 8/2020 Art 4.3 | Zero fee if refinancing within same lending entity. |
| **Partial Prepayment Fee** | Max 1.0% of prepaid sum or AED 10,000 (whichever is lower) | Circular 8/2020 Art 4.3 | One prepayment per quarter permitted without exit penalty. |
| **Late Payment Penalty Fee** | Max AED 150 per delinquent cycle | Notice 2011/23 Art 9.2 | No compounding of delinquent interest permitted. |
| **Loan Restructuring / Rescheduling Fee** | Max AED 250 | Circular 8/2020 Art 4.2 | Free if restructuring due to documented job loss or financial distress. |
| **Post-Dated Cheque Replacement Fee** | Max AED 100 per instance | Standard Retail Fee Schedule | Maximum aggregate capped at AED 500 per annual cycle. |
| **Credit Card Cash Advance Fee** | Max 3.0% of cash advance or AED 100 (whichever is higher) | Notice 2011/23 Art 8 | Interest accrues from transaction date without grace period. |
| **Liability / No-Liability Certificate** | Max AED 60 per letter | Circular 8/2020 Art 4.2 | Must be issued within 7 business days of request. |
