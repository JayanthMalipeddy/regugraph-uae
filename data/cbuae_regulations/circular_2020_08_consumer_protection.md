# Central Bank of the United Arab Emirates (CBUAE)
## Circular No. 8/2020: Consumer Protection Regulation & Standards (CPS)

### Article 1: Legislative Authority and Scope
1.1. These Regulations are issued pursuant to the powers vested in the Central Bank under Decretal Federal Law No. (14) of 2018 Regarding the Central Bank & Organization of Financial Institutions and Activities.
1.2. The provisions of this Regulation and its accompanying Standards apply to all Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs) operating in the State of the UAE, including commercial banks, Islamic banks, finance companies, and peer-to-peer lending platforms.

---

### Article 4: Credit & Retail Lending Conduct and Disclosure

#### Section 4.1: Key Facts Statement (KFS)
4.1.1. Licensed Financial Institutions must provide a standardized Key Facts Statement (KFS) to the consumer prior to signing any credit agreement.
4.1.2. The KFS must explicitly highlight:
   - The total amount of credit and total repayable amount.
   - The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) calculated on a reducing balance basis.
   - All applicable one-off and recurring fees, insurance premiums, and third-party administrative charges.
   - The cooling-off period of five (5) business days during which the borrower can withdraw from the contract without penalty.

#### Section 4.2: Maximum Limits on Fees and Charges (Fee Caps)
4.2.1. LFIs are prohibited from charging retail consumers fees exceeding the statutory ceilings established by the Central Bank:
   - **Loan Processing / Facility Fee:** Shall not exceed **1.0%** of the loan amount or a maximum of **AED 2,500** (exclusive of VAT), whichever is lower.
   - **Late Payment Fee:** Shall not exceed **AED 150** per late installment cycle. No compounding of late payment penalties is permitted.
   - **Loan Rescheduling / Restructuring Fee:** Shall not exceed **AED 250**.
   - **Statement of Account Fee:** Free electronically; maximum **AED 25** for printed certified physical statements.

#### Section 4.3: Early Settlement and Prepayment Caps
4.3.1. In accordance with Article 4.3 of the Consumer Protection Standards, where a consumer partially or fully settles a retail credit facility prior to its contractual maturity:
   - The early settlement fee for personal, auto, and home loans shall be capped at a maximum of **1.0%** of the outstanding principal balance, or **AED 10,000**, whichever is lower.
   - Any clause demanding future unearned interest, penalty exit premiums exceeding 1%, or minimum fee thresholds exceeding AED 10,000 is deemed **null, void, and unenforceable** under UAE Federal Law.
4.3.2. Where early settlement is funded through internal restructuring within the same lending institution, no early settlement fee shall be levied.

---

### Article 7: Mandatory Transparency and Prohibition of Unfair Terms
7.1. Contractual terms that impose unilateral rate hikes without sixty (60) days prior written notice are strictly prohibited.
7.2. "Balloon payment" mechanisms that mask the true Debt Burden Ratio or artificially defer principal repayments beyond statutory limits are unlawful for retail consumer facilities.
