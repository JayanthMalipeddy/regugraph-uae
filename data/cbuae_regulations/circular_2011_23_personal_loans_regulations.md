# Central Bank of the United Arab Emirates (CBUAE)
## Regulations Regarding Bank Loans & Other Services Offered to Individual Customers (Notice No. 2011/23)

### Article 2: Personal Loans to Individuals

#### Section 2.1: Definition and Maximum Principal Amount
2.1.1. A Personal Loan is defined as an installment loan provided to an individual borrower to cover personal or consumer expenses, repaid through regular monthly deductions from the borrower's verified salary or end-of-service benefits.
2.1.2. The maximum principal amount of a personal consumer loan granted to any individual borrower shall **not exceed twenty (20) times the gross monthly salary** of the borrower.
2.1.3. Banks and finance companies are strictly prohibited from granting top-up loans that cause total aggregate debt to exceed the 20x gross salary ceiling.

#### Section 2.2: Maximum Repayment Tenure
2.2.1. The maximum permissible repayment tenure for any personal loan facility shall **not exceed forty-eight (48) months** (4 years) from the date of initial disbursement.
2.2.2. Exceptions are granted exclusively to armed forces personnel and defense ministry employees subject to separate bilateral armed forces protocols. For all civilian citizens and expatriates, any term exceeding 48 months represents a direct regulatory breach.

### Article 5: Car Loans (Vehicle Financing)
5.1. The maximum repayment tenure for any vehicle or auto financing loan granted to an individual borrower shall **not exceed sixty (60) months** (5 years).
5.2. Banks and finance companies must require a minimum down payment of **twenty percent (20%)** of the vehicle purchase invoice value. Financing 100% of a vehicle's purchase price without down payment is strictly forbidden.

---

### Article 7: Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) Statutory Cap

#### Section 7.1: The 50% Rule
7.1.1. The aggregate monthly debt repayment obligations of any retail borrower—including personal loan EMIs, auto loan EMIs, mortgage installments, and 5% of all credit card credit limits—shall **never exceed fifty percent (50%) of the borrower's gross monthly income**.
7.1.2. **Formula:**
$$DBR = \frac{\sum (\text{All Monthly Loan Installments}) + 0.05 \times \sum (\text{Credit Card Limits})}{\text{Gross Monthly Salary}} \times 100 \le 50.0\%$$
7.1.3. For pensioners and retirees, the maximum allowable DBR ceiling is further restricted to **thirty percent (30%)** of their validated monthly pension income.

#### Section 7.2: Sanctions for DBR Violations
7.2.1. If an LFI approves credit that breaches the 50% DBR threshold, the Central Bank may mandate the lending bank to write off the interest component and reschedule the remaining principal without penalties.
