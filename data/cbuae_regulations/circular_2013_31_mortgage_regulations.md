# Central Bank of the United Arab Emirates (CBUAE)
## Mortgage Loans Regulations (Circular No. 31/2013)

### Article 3: Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios

#### Section 3.1: UAE Nationals (First vs Subsequent Homes)
3.1.1. For UAE Citizens purchasing their first residential home:
   - Where property value is **up to AED 5 million**: Maximum LTV is **85%** (Minimum 15% down payment).
   - Where property value **exceeds AED 5 million**: Maximum LTV is **75%** (Minimum 25% down payment).
3.1.2. For UAE Citizens purchasing subsequent properties or investment homes:
   - Maximum LTV is capped at **65%** regardless of property valuation.

#### Section 3.2: Non-UAE Nationals / Expatriates
3.2.1. For Expatriate residents purchasing their first residential home:
   - Where property value is **up to AED 5 million**: Maximum LTV is **80%** (Minimum 20% down payment).
   - Where property value **exceeds AED 5 million**: Maximum LTV is **70%** (Minimum 30% down payment).
3.2.2. For Expatriates purchasing second or subsequent residential properties:
   - Maximum LTV is strictly capped at **60%** (Minimum 40% down payment).

#### Section 3.3: Off-Plan Properties
3.3.1. For all off-plan property purchases (whether by UAE Nationals or Expatriates):
   - Maximum LTV across all institutions is capped at **50%**.

### Article 6: Maximum Mortgage Tenure and Borrower Age Limit
6.1. The maximum repayment period for any residential mortgage facility shall **not exceed twenty-five (25) years** (300 months).
6.2. The maximum age of the borrower at the date of the final scheduled installment:
   - For UAE Nationals: Shall not exceed **70 years**.
   - For Expatriate Residents: Shall not exceed **65 years** (or regular retirement age, whichever is lower, unless guaranteed business cash flows exist).

---

### Article 8: Early Prepayment Caps on Home Loans
8.1. Consistent with Central Bank Notice 3877/2019 amending mortgage early settlement terms:
   - The fee for early partial or full exit of a residential mortgage facility shall not exceed **1% of the prepaid principal balance** or **AED 10,000**, whichever is less.
   - Any bank attempting to charge higher fixed fees or calculate percentages on the original loan balance is in direct breach of Central Bank standards.
